## Общий запрос

Use case: precise-object-edit. Image 1 is the edit target photograph. Image 2 is the exact geometric logo from Fox Coffee packaging, provided as an insert/reference. Replace ONLY the existing fox emblem on the front of the cup/glass in image 1 with the identical solid dark espresso-brown symbol in image 2. Exact silhouette: curved pointed top and wide pointed sides, two small triangular notches at the bottom, rounded low center. This is an abstract logo, NOT a realistic fox: NO eyes, NO nose, NO legs, NO tail, NO extra details. Use the symbol alone, without the cream rectangular background. Small flat screen-printed emblem approx same size and at same position as existing logo, naturally following curvature and lighting. Preserve all other pixels/composition as much as possible: original portrait aspect ratio, original cup/glass/handle, beverage, foam pattern or garnish, saucer, stone tabletop, green sofa, light, background. No other objects, no lettering, no watermark. Output one edited product photo.
