# Отдельные фотографии продуктов

Созданы встроенным инструментом image_gen. Финальные фотографии находятся в этой папке в формате WebP.

## cappuccino.webp

Use case: product-mockup. Asset: individual product photograph for Fox Coffee menu. Photograph one single ivory ceramic cup of cappuccino with beautiful latte art on a matching saucer. ONLY this one food or drink product, no other food, drinks or cups. Cozy walnut coffee shop table, warm natural side window light, softly blurred dark olive brown café background. Premium photorealistic editorial food photography, natural tactile textures, elegant restrained composition. Portrait 4:5 framing, product entirely visible centered in middle with generous negative space around it so both portrait and wide crops work. Camera three-quarter view. No text, logo, watermark, people, hands or artificial steam.
